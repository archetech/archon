Anchored in a Block

An animated walkthrough of how Archon registers operations on blockchains: choosing a registry, the per-registry queue, cutting a batch whose DID commits to its operations, anchoring it in one transaction on Bitcoin, Zcash, Ethereum or Solana, importing it on every node, and the confirmation and timestamps that result, including what an orphaned block undoes. Everything on screen is drawn live with canvas code.
