Letters in a Vault

An animated walkthrough of dmail, Archon’s encrypted mail between DIDs: writing a message, sealing it in a vault whose key is wrapped for each recipient, sending it with an expiring notice, how a wallet finds and files its mail, replies, Herald’s bridge to and from email, and what stays public or private. Everything on screen is drawn live with canvas code.
